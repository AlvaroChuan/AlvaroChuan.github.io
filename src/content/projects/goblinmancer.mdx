## Summary

**GoblinMancer** is a tower-defense auto-battler and roguelite developed by **BackToBits Studio**. 

Taking on the role of an outcast goblin necromancer defending their sanctum, players slay advancing waves of human heroes and resurrect their fallen corpses into an ever-growing, synergistic undead army. The game merges automatic combat simulation with active tactical spellcasting and deep roguelite meta-progression.

## Responsibilities

* **Swarm Navigation & Flowfield Pathfinding:** Engineered a high-density flowfield navigation grid and local boid separation system, allowing hundreds of active combatants to smoothly traverse tight dungeon chokepoints at a stable 60+ FPS without standard NavMesh overhead.

## Current Status & Reception

* **Steam Store & Itch Prototype:** Listed on Steam with playable prototype builds validated through community game jam playtests.
* **Active Production:** In full production at BackToBits Studio, actively redesing the core architecture to work with Unity ECS.
* **Player Reception:** Strongly received during early playtesting sessions for its satisfying "snowballing horde" gameplay loop and chaotic large-scale battles.
