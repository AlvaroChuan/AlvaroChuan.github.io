## Summary

**Step By Step** is a narrative graphic adventure game developed by **Hike Squad** in official collaboration with the **Town Hall of Ares del Maestrat** (Castellón, Spain) and Universitat Jaume I.

The game follows a young protagonist traversing the historical *Ruta dels Molins d'Ares del Maestrat* (The Watermill's Route) to fulfill their grandmother's final wish: carrying her ashes to the scenic waterfall of El Salt. Designed to promote cultural tourism and preserve regional heritage, the game blends heartfelt narrative storytelling with educational minigames rooted in real historical watermills.

## Responsibilities

* **Full-Stack Game Architecture:** Led the software development and engineering of the entire game, handling game state management, save/load routines, camera framing, and audio coordination.
* **Historical Minigame Mechanics:** Implemented several interactive minigames recreating traditional rural trades and machinery, including watermill stone realignment, sluice gate flow regulation, and river navigation.
* **Dialogue & Quest Logic:** Built an interactive branching dialogue interpreter supporting localized Spanish/Valencian scripts, character portrait mood switching, and narrative quest item checks.
* **On-Site Research & Client Collaboration:** Participated in on-site field research in Ares del Maestrat, coordinating directly with municipal historians and council representatives to ensure precise cultural and geographic fidelity.

## Current Status & Reception

* **Municipal Endorsement:** Officially reviewed, approved, and utilized by the Town Hall of Ares del Maestrat as an interactive digital tourism asset for promoting local tourism.
* **Academic Honors:** Received the highest grade in the collaborative Triple Subject Project evaluation at Universitat Jaume I.
* **Public Release:** Available for download on [itch.io](https://hollowblink.itch.io/stepbystep).
