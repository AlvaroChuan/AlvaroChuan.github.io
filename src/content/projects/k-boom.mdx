## Summary

**K-Boom!** is an explosive grid-based puzzle game developed and published by **BackToBits Studio**, featuring a playable demo and upcoming commercial release on **Steam**. 

Players guide Camilo the chameleon through hazardous test chambers by strategically planting directional bombs, triggering chain reactions, and manipulating movable obstacles. The gameplay blends rigorous spatial puzzle logic with high-impact arcade game feel.

## Responsibilities

* **Coding team leader:** Coordinated and revised all code implementations of the game while making sure the systems where finished accorded to our timelines.
* **Dialogue system:** Crafted and integrated the whole dialogue system of the game using .ink files and tag parsing to trigger control the dialogue flow.
* **Controller support:** Coded the whole controller support system and UI glyph changes to accomodate controller players.
* **Player controller & kinematics:** Crafted responsive movement mechanics.
* **Puzzle elements:** Coded several puzzle elements and their intended reactions to the bombs.
* **Custom production tooling:** Architected and engineered the custom in-engine level editor used by the design team to produce, validate, and tune all puzzle chambers in the game.

## Current Status & Reception

* **Steam page & Public demo:** Published on Steam to gather wishlists with a publicly playable demo (**K-Boom! Demo**).
* **Festival Showcases:** Exhibited at major Spanish video game events, including **SAGA (Saló del Gaming de Barcelona)** and the **Guerrilla Game Festival**, drawing long queues of playtesters.
* **Player Reception:** Praised by players and event attendees for its responsive feel, polished audiovisual juice, and satisfying puzzle design curve.
