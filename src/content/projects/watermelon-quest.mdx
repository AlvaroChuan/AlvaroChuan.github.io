## Summary

**Watermelon Quest** is a vibrant 2D precision platformer developed as Álvaro's introductory game project during his first year at **Universitat Jaume I (UJI)** for the *Basic Information and Communication Technologies* course.

The game puts players in the shoes of a determined protagonist tasked with helping a distressed stranger recover all his lost watermelons, which have been scattered across increasingly perilous obstacle courses. Players must master timing, jumping arcs, and environmental hazards to safely retrieve every fruit.

## Responsibilities

* **2D Kinematic Controller:** Programmed a responsive platformer movement controller in Unity featuring variable jump heights, coyote time, jump buffering, and responsive aerial drag.
* **Environmental Hazards & Traps:** Engineered interactive level mechanics including spike colliders, moving floating platforms with smooth interpolation, and collectible trigger zones.
* **Level Design & Tilemapping:** Designed and constructed sequential platforming challenges using Unity's 2D Tilemap tools, structuring a steady difficulty curve that introduces mechanics progressively.
* **Game Loop & State Flow:** Established game loop state management, respawn checkpoint persistence, fruit inventory tallies, and level completion victory screens.

## Current Status & Reception

* **Academic Milestone:** Successfully presented and graded with the highest marks in the first-year university computer science curriculum at Universitat Jaume I.
* **Open Source Repository:** Project source code, assets, and documentation preserved on [GitHub](https://github.com/AlvaroChuan/Watermelon-Quest) as a personal milestone documenting the start of Álvaro's game engineering journey.
