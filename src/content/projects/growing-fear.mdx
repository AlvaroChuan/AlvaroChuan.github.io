## Summary

**Growing Fear** is an eerie agricultural management game crafted in 48 hours for the **UJI Game Jam 2023 Spooky Edition** by **BackToBits**.

Subverting classic cozy farming conventions, players cultivate bizarre haunted crops to accumulate supernatural capital, expand their farmland plots, and ship frightful produce to nearby cities. As shipments arrive, town fear levels surge until the player establishes eerie dominion over the entire planet.

## Responsibilities

* **Grid Farming State Machine:** Designed and coded the farm plot interaction architecture, managing soil tilling, seed hydration, germination timers, multi-stage growth cycles, and harvesting events.
* **Economic Progression Loop:** Engineered the balancing curves governing crop yield values, expansion land acquisition costs, and resource re-investment thresholds to maintain satisfying pacing.
* **Planetary Fear System:** Programmed settlement supply-and-demand mechanics where harvesting specific crop tiers inflicts fear increments upon regional towns, driving macro-level progression.
* **UI Feedback & Audio Hooks:** Integrated contextual plot inspection overlays, harvesting audio cues, currency counters, and dynamic warning prompts for neglected crops.
