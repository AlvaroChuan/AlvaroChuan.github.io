## Summary

**Prop Trip** is a fast-paced first-person movement shooter created in 48 hours for **UJI Game Jam 2024** as a joint collaboration between **BackToBits** and **Punic Goose**.

Trapped in an infinite vertical arena, players must survive escalating onslaughts of hostile adversaries by stringing together high-velocity slides, aerial bunny hops, and pinpoint projectile shots. The project focused on pushing stylized cel-shaded rendering and responsive first-person movement kinematics under rapid prototyping constraints.

## Responsibilities

* **Level design:** Designed and validated the main game level. 
* **Stylized Cel-Shading Pipeline:** Developed custom Universal Render Pipeline (URP) toon ramp shaders, Sobel outline detection, radial speed lines, and post-processing color grading to create an expressive comic-book aesthetic.
* **Gunplay & Bullet-Hell Dynamics:** Engineered hitscan and projectile weapons, weapon kickback sway, particle muzzle flashes, and dense enemy bullet-pattern spawning logic.
* **Dynamic Wave Scaling:** Designed an infinite wave spawning system that monitors player survival times and kill velocity to continuously adapt enemy squad compositions and spawn frequencies.

## Current Status & Reception

* **Game Jam Submission:** Completed and delivered within the strict 48-hour timeline of UJI Game Jam 2024.
* **Player Feedback:** Highly praised by fellow jam creators and players for its fluid momentum-based movement mechanics and distinctive cartoon action aesthetic.
* **Public Build:** Jam edition available for download on [itch.io](https://punicgoose.itch.io/prop-trip), with ongoing iterations planned for extended release.
